🔴 AGGREGATIONS & ANNOTATIONS (36–42)

36. Get total number of books.
--> Book.objects.count()

37. Get average price of all books.
    from django.db.models import Avg, Max, Min
--> Book.objects.aggregate(avg_price = Avg("price"))

38. Get maximum book price.
❌ Incorrect--> Book.objects.aggregate(price=MAX(price))
✅ Correct Answer--> Book.objects.aggregate(max_price = Max("price"))

39. Get minimum book price.
❌ Incorrect--> Book.objects.aggregate(price=MIN(price))
✅ Correct Answer-->Book.objects.aggregate(min_price=Min("price"))

40. Get sum of prices of all books.
❌Incorrect--> Book.objects.aggregate(total = SUM(price))
✅Correct Answer--> Book.objects.aggregate(total_price=Sum("price"))

41. Get average book price per author.
❌ Incorrect--> Author.objects.aggregate(avg_price=AVG(book_set.price))
✅Correct Answer--> Author.objects.annotate(avg_price = Avg("book__price"))


42. Get authors ordered by number of books (descending).
❌ Incorrect--> Author.objects.aggregate(book_count=Count(book_set.price)).order_by("-book_count")
✅Correct Answer--> Author.objects.annotate(book_count=Count("book")).order_by("-book_count")
