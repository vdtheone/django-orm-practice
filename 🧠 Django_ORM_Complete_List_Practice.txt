1️⃣ QuerySet Basics (FOUNDATION)
===============================

1️⃣ Get top 5 most expensive active products.
--> Product.objects.filter(is_active=True).order_by("-price")[:5]

2️⃣ Check if any user exists with email ending in @gmail.com.
--> User.objects.filter(email__endswith="@gmail.com").exists()

3️⃣ Get unique city names from users table.
--> User.objects.values("city").distinct()

4️⃣ Get the second page of products (10 per page).
--> Product.objects.all()[10:20]

5️⃣ Fetch only id and name of inactive users ordered by created_at.
--> User.objects.filter(active=False).order_by("-created_at").values("id","name")

2️⃣ Field Lookups
================

1️⃣ Get users whose username starts with "admin" (case-insensitive).
--> User.objects.filter(username__istartswith="admin")

2️⃣ Fetch orders created in November 2024.
--> Order.objects.filter(created_at__month=11, created_at__year=2024)
--> Order.objects.filter(created_at__date__range(date(2024, 11, 1), date(2024, 11, 30)))

3️⃣ Get products priced greater than 1000 but less than 5000.
--> Product.objects.filter(price__range=(1000, 5000))
--> Product.objects.filter(price__gt=1000, price__lt=5000)

4️⃣ Find books whose title contains either "AI" or "ML" (case-insensitive).
--> Book.objects.filter(Q(title__icontains="AI") | Q(title__icontains="ML"))
💡 Cleaner alternative (regex):
--> Book.objects.filter(title__iregex=r"(ai|ml)")

5️⃣ Get users whose email is not null and ends with @company.com.
--> User.objects.filter(email__isnull=False, email__iendswith="@company.com")

3️⃣ Relationship Traversal (Joins)
=================================

1️⃣ Get users who have ordered a book priced above 2000.
--> User.objects.filter(orders__books__price__gt=2000).distinct()

2️⃣ Get authors who have no books.
---> Author.objects.filter(books__isnull=True)
✅ Optional alternative (annotation-based, interview-friendly):
---> Author.objects.annotate(book_count=Count("reviews")).filter(book_count=0)

3️⃣ Get orders placed by users whose email ends with @gmail.com.
---> Order.objects.filter(user__email__iendswith="@gmail.com")

4️⃣ Get books reviewed by users from India.
---> Book.objects.filter(reviews__user__country="India").distinct()

5️⃣ Get users who have reviewed more than 10 books.
---> User.objects.annotate(review_count=Count("reviews", distinct=True)).filter(review_count__gt=10)

4️⃣ Aggregation
==============

1️⃣ Get total number of active users.
--> User.objects.filter(is_active=True).aggregate(total_active_user=Count("id"))

2️⃣ Get average order amount for completed orders.
--> Order.objects.filter(status="completed").aggregate(avg_order_amount=Avg(total_amount))

3️⃣ Get maximum price of books written by authors from India.
--> Book.objects.filter(author__country="India").aggregate(max_price=Max("price"))

4️⃣ Get total revenue generated by each author’s books (⚠️ think before choosing aggregate or annotate).
--> Author.objects.annotate(total_revenue=Sum(F("books__order_items__quantity") * F("books__price")))

5️⃣ Get minimum and maximum order amount placed by a specific user.
--> Order.objects.filter(user_id=user_id).aggregate(min_amount=Min("total_amount"), max_amount=Max("total_amount"))

5️⃣ Annotation
=============

1️⃣ Annotate each author with number of books priced above 1000.
--> Author.objects.annotate(number_of_books = Count("books", filter=Q("books__price__gt=1000")))

2️⃣ Get books annotated with total number of reviews.
--> Book.objects.annotate(total_review=Count("reviews"))

3️⃣ Get authors who have average book price greater than 500.
--> Author.objects.annotate(avg_book_price=Avg("books__price")).filter(avg_book_price__gt=500)

4️⃣ Annotate users with total order amount they have spent.
--> User.objects.annotate(
        total_spent_amount = Sum(
            ExpressionWrapper(
                F("orders__order_items__quantity) * F("orders__order_items__price"),
                output_field = DecimalField()
            )
        )
    )

5️⃣ Get top 5 authors ordered by total revenue generated.
--> Author.objects.annotate(
        total_revenue=Sum(
            ExpressionWrapper(
                F("books__order_items__quantity) * F("books__price"),
                output_field=DecimalField()
            )
        )
    ).order_by("-total_revenue")[:5]

6️⃣ Expressions (Very Important)
===============================

1️⃣ Increase all book prices by 5%
--> Book.objects.update(price=F("price") * 1.05)

2️⃣ Mark orders as delayed if shipped after expected date
--> Order.objects.annotate(
        is_delayed=Case(
            When(shipped_at__gt=F("expected_delivery_date"), then=True),
            default=False,
            output_field=BooleanField()
        )
    )

3️⃣ Deduct stock atomically when order is created
--> Book.objects.filter(id=book_id).update(
        stock=F("stock") - ordered_quantity
    )

4️⃣ Annotate users with loyalty level using total spend
--> User.objects.annotate(
        total_spent_amount = Sum(
            ExpressionWrapper(
                F("orders__order_items__quantity") *
                F("orders__order_items__price"),
                output_field=DecimalField()
            )
        ),
        loyalty_level=Case(
            When(total_spent__gte=100000, then="Platinum"),
            When(total_spent__gte=50000, then="Gold"),
            When(total_spent__gte=20000, then="Silver"),
            default="Bronze",
            output_field=CharField()
        )
    )

5️⃣ Find products where discount_price > selling_price
--> Product.objects.filter(discounted_price__gt=F("price"))
