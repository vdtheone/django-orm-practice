2️⃣2️⃣ Get total quantity sold per book.

✅ Correct Answer ---> Book.objects.annotate(total_quantity=Count("order_items__quentity))

2️⃣3️⃣ Get average book price per author.
---> Author.objects.annotate(avg_price=Avg("books__price")).distinct()

2️⃣4️⃣ Get books with total sales value greater than 50,000.
❌ Incorrect ---> Book.objects.annotate(total_sales=Sum(total_amount)).filter(total_sales__gt=50000)
✅ Correct Answer ---> Book.objects.annotate(total_sales=Sum(F("order_items__quantity") * F("price"))).filter(total_sales__gt=50000)

2️⃣5️⃣ Get authors ordered by total revenue generated.
❌ Incorrect ---> Author.objects.annotate(total_revenue=Sum(books_orders__total_amount)).order_by("total_revenue")
✅ Correct Answer ---> Author.objects.annotate(total_revenue=Sum(F("books__order_items__quentity) * F("books__price))).order_by("-total_revenue")

2️⃣6️⃣ Get users who have placed more than 5 orders.
---> User.objects.annotate(order_count = Count("orders")).filter(order_count__gt=5)

2️⃣7️⃣ Get top 3 most reviewed books.
❌ Incorrect ---> Book.objects.order_by("-books__review")[:3]
✅ Correct Answer ---> Book.objects.annotate(review_count = Count("review")).order_by("-review_count")[:3]

2️⃣8️⃣ Annotate each book with average rating.
❌ Incorrect ---> Book.objects.annotate(avg_rating=Avg(books__review__rating))
✅ Correct Answer ---> Book.objects.annotate(avg_rating=Avg("review__rating"))
