🟣 RELATIONSHIPS & JOINS (26–35)

26. Get all books along with their authors (optimized).
--> Book.objects.select_related("author").all()

27. Get all books written by author name = “Vishnu”.
--> Book.objects.select_related("author").filter(author__name="Vishnu")


28. Get all authors who have written at least one book.
❌--> Author.objects.prefetch_related("book_set")
----> prefetch_related() does not filter
----> You must use annotate + filter

    from django.db.models import Count
✅--> Author.objects.annotate(book_count=Count('book')).filter(book_count__gt=0)
✅--> Author.objects.filter(book__isnull=False).distinct()

29. Get all authors who have NOT written any book.
❌--> No Idea
✅-(recommended)-> Author.objects.annotate(book_count=Count('book')).filter(book_count=0)
✅--> Author.objects.filter(book__isnull=True)

30. Get total number of books per author.
❌ Incorrect--> Book.objects.aggregate("author_count" = author_id.count())
✅ Correct Answer--> Author.objects.annotate(book_count=Count('book'))
    📌 Each Author objects gets book_count.

31. Get authors with more than 5 books.
❌ Incorrect--> Author.objects.aggregate("book_set".count()> 5)
✅ Correct Answer--> Author.objects.annotate(book_count=Count("book")).filter(book_count__gt=5)

32. Get all books and print author name without N+1 queries.
--> books = Book.objects.select_related("author")
    for book in books:
        print(book.author.name)

33. Get latest book of each author.
--> latest_book = Book.objects.filter(author=OuterRef('pk')).order_by('-published')
    Author.objects.annotate(latest_book_title=latest_book.values("title")[:1])

34. Get books where author name starts with “A”.
❌--> No Idea
✅ Correct Answer-->Book.objects.filter(author__name__startswith="A")

35. Get all authors along with their books (reverse relation).
❌--> No Idea
✅ Correct Answer--> Author.objects.prefetch_related("book_set")
