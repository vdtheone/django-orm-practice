22. Update price of book with id=5 to 900.
--> book = Book.objects.get(id=5)
    book.price = 900
    book.save()
--> Book.objects.filter(id=5).update(price=900)

23. Increase price of all books by 10%.
❌--> Book.objects.update(price=price*100/100+10)
✅--> correct answere ---->>    
        from django.db.models import F
        Book.objects.update(price=F("price") * 1.10)


24. Delete all books cheaper than 200.
--> Book.objects.filter(price__lt=200).delete()
