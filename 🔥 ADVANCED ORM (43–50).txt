43. Get books that are the most expensive among all books.
❌ Incorrect--> Book.objects.aggregate(most_expensive=Max("price"))
✅ Correct Answer--> max_price = Book.objects.aggregate(max_price = Max("price"))["max_price"]
                     Book.objects.filter(price=max_price)

44. Get authors whose latest book price is above 1000.
❌ Incorrect--> Author.objects.filter(book_price__gt=1000).order_by("-book_published")
✅ Correct Answer--> 
                    latest_book = Book.objects.filter(
                        author=OuterRef("pk")
                    ).order_by("-published")

                    Author.objects.annotate(
                        latest_price=Subquery(latest_book.values("price")[:1])
                    ).filter(latest_price__gt=1000)


45. Get books published in the same year as their author’s first book.
❌ Incorrect--> Book.objects.filter(published__year=2025).distinct()
✅ Correct Answer-->
                    first_book_year = Book.objects.filter(
                        author=OuterRef("author")
                    ).values("author").annotate(
                        first_year=Min("published")
                    ).values("first_year__year)

                    Book.objects.filter(published__year=Subquery(first_book_year))

46. Use F() expression to compare price with another field.
❌ Incorrect--> No Idea
✅ Correct Answer--> Book.objects.filter(price__gt=F("discount_price"))


47. Use Exists() to get authors having books priced above 800.
❌ Incorrect--> Author.objects.filter(book_price__gt=500).exists()
✅ Correct Answer-->
                    expensive_books = Book.objects.filter(
                        author = OuterRef("pk"),
                        price__gt = 800
                    )
                    Author.objects.filter(Exists(expensive_books))
